## Removed after production-source audit

- `.screen-title__row` and `.launcher-header__badge`; the current React launcher already renders the accessible `h1`/subtitle structure without these retired hooks.
- `.mini-ledger`, `.mini-ledger__row`, `.ledger-list`, `.ledger-row`, `.ledger-row__country`, `.ledger-note`, `.mastery-list` presentation remnants.
- `.status-chip*` and retired `.status-text*` variants.
- `.filter-tabs` / `.filter-tab*` remnants.
- `.progress-achievement*` remnants.
- `.flag-frame--ledger` remnants.
- `.map-viewport-controls` / `.map-viewport-control*` remnants.
- `.test-advance` remnants.

These families had no current production TS/TSX consumer at closeout outside their style definitions. Test-only references are not treated as production usage.

## Retained intentionally

The conventional launcher remains valid renderer/WebGL fallback infrastructure. Production-used fallback selectors including `.page--launcher`, `.launcher-header__icon`, `.launcher__scope-list` and `.launcher__learn` remain in place.

The #149 accessibility work is retained: the current React launcher keeps its focusable labelled `h1` and subtitle structure, and this cleanup does not reintroduce the retired badge wrapper.

## Shared control ownership

Exact duplicate `.text-icon-button` base/state/responsive/reduced-motion rules now have one durable owner in `src/styles/styles.css`; the duplicate Atlas-theme ownership is removed. `.icon-button` remains separately owned where its geometry differs.

## Closeout verification contract

- `npm run check`
- full `npm test`
- representative exact-production browser regressions for Spatial, launcher/fallback, quiz and Profile on Chromium/mobile Chromium
- inspect generated production `dist/` and confirm retired selectors are absent while required fallback styling remains

Temporary #151 execution workflows/scripts used during the audit and acceptance are deliberately excluded from the shipped repository.
