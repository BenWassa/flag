# Atlas Gamification

**Status:** current production contract

Atlas uses **scarce recognition**, not an activity economy. It rewards demonstrated geographic competence without XP farming, currency collection, lives, reward shops, fantasy ranks or badge spam.

## Hierarchy

`live country evidence → region × domain Mastery → complete region → complete continent → World Crown`

Country evidence is operational learning state. Prestige begins at region × domain.

## Ordinary progress

Routine progress is intentionally quiet: the Atlas Blue successful-retrieval strip. The dedicated Progress screen is retired; progress is disclosed in the current Spatial/navigation/activity/results flow rather than a separate dashboard.

## Perfect round

A Perfect round is one Play result with no misses under the domain's native scoring rules. It receives transient gold acknowledgement on Results and creates no persistent achievement.

## Region × domain Mastery

Current behaviour:

- only qualifying region-scoped Play can feed the Mastery streak;
- exact complete supported-target coverage is verified (#108);
- perfect qualifying round increments the streak;
- qualifying non-perfect round resets an unearned streak;
- two consecutive perfect qualifying rounds award Mastery;
- earned Mastery is not revoked;
- Learn, Review, continent Play and World Play do not award region Mastery.

## Complete region

Requires genuine non-empty curriculum in all four domains plus all four region × domain Masteries. Presentation keeps useful progress/context and adds restrained gold plus explicit non-colour semantics. No separate region medal/shield/Crown.

## Complete continent

Requires every required learner-facing region to be complete. State persists and has dedicated crest/trophy artwork. Spatial and fallback navigation consume the same completion read model; neither owns qualification.

## World Crown

World completion is the highest prestige tier. All six continents now meet the curriculum gate, but the Crown is earned only after all six actual continent-completion achievements.

Issue #138 shipped the earned-only learner-facing Home surface. Do not show locked decorative Crowns, duplicate it routinely through navigation, or introduce a tier above it.

## Persistence

Earned Mastery/completion is monotonic under the current product model. Country evidence may later lapse or become due without revoking historical earned prestige. In-progress perfect-run streaks persist separately.

## What Atlas deliberately does not use

No XP, currencies, lives, reward stores, arbitrary daily-streak rewards, fantasy rank ladders, leaderboards, routine confetti, ordinary-progress Crowns, country-level Mastered prestige or numerically dense completion dashboards.

## Presentation rules

- ordinary action/progress: Atlas Blue;
- correct/wrong: green/red;
- persistent region × domain Mastery: purple + non-colour cue;
- complete region: restrained gold;
- complete continent: crest/trophy completion state;
- World Crown: singular highest tier.

Prestige becomes more visually distinctive as rarity increases, not more numerically dense.
