# Research Archive

This section preserves exploratory research, original design artifacts, quantitative studies and rationale/history that informed Atlas. It is **not** the source of current product truth.

Accepted product decisions belong in `docs/product/`, root `PRODUCT.md`, root `DESIGN.md`, and `docs/architecture/` where appropriate.

## Archives

- [`gamification/`](gamification/) — gamification philosophy, scarcity/prestige reasoning, mastery hierarchy exploration, and the original gamification design brief.
- [`flag-colour-study/`](flag-colour-study/) — quantitative study of the colour vocabulary of the canonical 195-country flag curriculum, including original CSV outputs, methodology, accessibility checks and the brand interpretation that led to Atlas Blue.

## Evidence chain

Research should make the relationship explicit:

**research evidence → accepted product/design decision → implementation**

When a decision changes later, preserve the research evidence here and update the normative product/design record rather than rewriting historical results.
